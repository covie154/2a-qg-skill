## Question

A 34-year-old premenopausal woman undergoes pelvic US for chronic pelvic discomfort. A
2.4-cm unilocular adnexal cyst is identified with anechoic contents, a thin smooth wall,
and no solid, papillary, or vascular components.

**What is the most likely diagnosis?**

A. Ovarian cystadenoma
B. Follicle cyst
C. Endometrioma
D. Corpus luteum cyst
E. Cystic ovarian malignancy

---

## Answer

**Correct option: B** — Follicle cyst

---

## Explanation

Simple cysts 3 cm or smaller in premenopausal women are almost certainly follicles — a
normal physiologic finding requiring no follow-up.[1][2] The SRU consensus update found
exceptionally low cancer risk associated with simple adnexal cysts, and sets the
premenopausal reporting threshold at 3 cm with follow-up imaging only above 5-7 cm.[2] The
thin smooth wall, anechoic contents, and absent flow match the source paper's definition
of a simple cyst.[1]

| Option | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A. Ovarian cystadenoma | Incorrect | The SRU update notes simple cysts that **grow** are likely cystadenomas; a small, simple, stable-appearing cyst in a 34-year-old is overwhelmingly physiologic, not neoplastic [2]. The paper reserves concern for solid or vascular components [1]. |
| B. Follicle cyst | **Correct** | Size ≤3 cm + simple morphology in a premenopausal patient is the paper's O-RADS 1 physiologic follicle [1]; corroborated by the SRU 3 cm reporting threshold [2]. |
| C. Endometrioma | Incorrect | Endometriomas characteristically produce homogeneous low-level internal echoes with a "ground glass" appearance on US [4]; the anechoic contents in this stem exclude it. |
| D. Corpus luteum cyst | Incorrect | The paper describes the classic corpus luteum as a thick wall with crenulated inner margins, internal echoes, and intense peripheral colour Doppler flow — none present here [1]. |
| E. Cystic ovarian malignancy | Incorrect | O-RADS stratifies risk into six categories (0-5) spanning normal to high risk of malignancy, the high-risk categories being defined by solid components and other worrisome features [3]; none are described. |
